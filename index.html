What is Project Graveyard?

Every year, students create amazing projects that never get finished. Maybe the team graduates, runs out of time, loses interest, or simply doesn't know how to continue.

Project Graveyard gives these abandoned ideas a second chance. Students can bury unfinished projects, discover projects created by others, and request to revive them.
